# 2026-11-27 — Graphics Quality Overhaul

## What changed

1. **8x Real-ESRGAN art & HD mipmaps (`59fa68d`):**
   - Rewrote `tools/upscale8x.py` to generate `assets/8x/` sprites, UI, and font directly from `original/sprites/` and `assets/ui/` instead of chaining pixel-art `xBR 4x -> xBR 2x` (which produced onion-ring alpha halos and painterly smearing on pre-rendered 3D art).
   - Sprites: 16-iter RGB edge-bleeding into transparent pixels, 16 px edge-replicated padding, two-pass `realesrgan-x4plus` downsampled via Lanczos to exact 8x, and hybrid alpha (`realesrgan-x4plus-anime` for hard silhouettes blended with smooth bicubic for feathered glows/shadows); tiny `<= 12 px` icons (`aim`, `bonus`, `indicator`, `shipCounter`, `bombCounter`) use clean Lanczos resampling.
   - UI & font: edge-bled `realesrgan-x4plus-anime` brought to 8x via Lanczos (`github.png` rendered directly from SVG at 128×128), losslessly compressed with `oxipng -o 2 --strip safe`.
   - `src/main.rs` & `src/hud.rs`: added `load_mipmapped` for trilinear mipmaps (`TextureWrap::Clamp`, skipping packed multi-frame atlases `rock4A`, `freeze4`, `cinder4`, `shyGuyFrames`, `bigBonus`, `ngage`), parallel worker-thread predecoding (`predecode_hd_assets` + `clear_predecoded`), linear filtering by default on the HD font atlas, and HD font loading in `load_pause_assets`.
2. **5-level pyramid bloom & ACES post-FX (`634f38f`):**
   - Replaced the single 1/4-res 2-pass Gaussian blur in `src/gfx.rs` with a 5-level progressive bloom pyramid (half-res down to 1/32-res) using a 13-tap energy-preserving downsample (`BLOOM_DOWN_FRAGMENT`) and additive 9-tap tent upsample (`BLOOM_UP_FRAGMENT`).
   - Fixed the `(BLOOM_STRENGTH * 255.0) as u8` saturation bug (`BLOOM_ADD_FRAGMENT` for non-post screens), bound `Bloom` directly into `POST_FRAGMENT` so shockwaves distort both the scene and glow, added linear-space bloom addition with calibrated Narkowicz ACES filmic tone mapping, and added 1-LSB dithering against dark-gradient banding.
3. **Planet surface, cloud shadows, molten lava & parallax starfield (`4e83224`):**
   - Upgraded `BACKDROP_VERTEX` / `BACKDROP_FRAGMENT` (`highp` UVs, `view_ndc` limb distance, `Time`, `WorldStyle`).
   - Added parallax cloud drop shadows on Worlds 1 & 3, ocean/water key + dynamic specular glint, animated pulsing molten magma fissures on World 2 (`fire` + `crust`), dramatic crater relief on Worlds 4 & 5, per-world atmospheric Fresnel limb haze, dynamic point-light scattering through clouds/fog, and a 3-layer twinkling procedural parallax starfield with diffraction spikes on World 6, Survival, and menus.
4. **Parallax ground shadows & per-pixel PBR sprite lighting (`d37c6fb`):**
   - Added single-batch parallax-offset soft drop shadows on planet worlds (Worlds 1–5) in `Play::draw_shadows`.
   - Upgraded `LIT_VERTEX` / `LIT_FRAGMENT` to per-pixel 3D dome + 4-tap luminance-bump normals (rotated into screen space via vertex-colour-encoded `(cos, sin, SpriteMat)` with zero extra normal-map VRAM or per-sprite uniform switches), shading every pixel with key light, hemisphere fill, all 12 dynamic point lights (diffuse + Blinn-Phong specular), material-aware Fresnel rim lighting (`Rock`, `Ice`, `Fire`, `Craft`, `Core`), and glowing molten fissures on fire rocks. Lit the player ship hull too, and kept text labels and boss health bars unlit.
5. **Procedural energy shaders & lit physics particles (`588236a`):**
   - Added `SHIELD_FRAGMENT` (spherical rotating hexagonal honeycomb Fresnel bubble) and `GRAVITY_FRAGMENT` (flowing cyan-violet tractor-beam chevrons + glowing plasma core) in `src/gfx.rs`, both feeding the bloom pyramid.
   - Upgraded `Play::draw_ship_fx` with a continuous tapered two-layer plasma afterburner ribbon (`u = 0.5` center-column strip, wrap-safe), weapon-coloured muzzle flashes, and upgraded warp boost lines.
   - Added cooling molten-ember cores on fresh scorch marks (`age < 3.5 s`) in `Play::draw_scorches`.
   - Upgraded `Bursts::draw` (`src/bursts.rs`) so billowing smoke puffs gather illumination from nearby dynamic point lights, and flying sparks render with white-hot tips tapering to glowing tails.
