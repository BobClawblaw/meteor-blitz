## Touch controls (modern-port item 8)

- Pushed 03bf8a0..2e56f17 (stats/achievements, web chip antialiasing) to nas and github.
- `src/touch.rs`: floating twin sticks on the original `wheelMove` art (left moves, right aims and fires, double tap for the gravity gun), a bomb button and boost by quick tap or a left/right button, following the original's `bomb_button`/`boost_control` settings. Presses are latched for the fixed-step simulation. Mouse fire is ignored while touch is in use, since macroquad turns touches into left clicks. `Canvas::to_canvas` maps window pixels to canvas units (shared with `Canvas::mouse`).
- `tools/web_screenshot.mjs`: `TOUCH=1` taps the X,Y points with touch events; `TOUCH=play` holds both sticks; `TOUCH_TAP=X,Y` taps a third finger while they are held.
- Verified (headless Chrome, swiftshader, emulated touch): menus to stage 1.1 by taps at 480x320 and 844x390; the ship moves right and fires up with both sticks held; a bomb tap while held destroys a rock; a tap on the pause icon opens the pause menu and hides the sticks. Desktop at 960x640: no overlay, autofire stage unchanged. `cargo test`: 38 passed.
- Next: try on a real phone; item 9 (Linux package, Steam Deck check).
