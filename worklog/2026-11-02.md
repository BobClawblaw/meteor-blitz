
## 8x backgrounds

- Problem: `assets/8x` copied the 4x set's 2x (1024x1024) terrain, so at 4K the backdrops were stretched about 1.9x and looked soft beside the 8x sprites. Measured: the 8x and 4x builds drew the same backdrops (game frames within 0.3 mean abs).
- `tools/upscale8x.py` now builds 4x terrain (2048x2048) from the originals with Real-ESRGAN x4plus, made to wrap the same way as the 4x set (bands doubled), and the normal maps from it. Real-ESRGAN was downloaded at the user's OK and installed at `~/tools/realesrgan/`; both upscale scripts default to it.
- `load_terrain` (main.rs): backdrop textures and normal maps are created through miniquad with repeat wrap, and with trilinear mipmaps in the 8x set. The first try (`glGenerateMipmap` on macroquad's textures) drew a black backdrop. The shader dropped `fract()`, which would draw a mipmap seam at the wrap.
- Verified at 3840x2160:
  - Fine detail is up 15-40% per world.
  - The row/column gradient scan finds no seam (its only spikes are the HUD text).
  - 60 fps, draw 0.69-0.75 ms (the 4x build 0.90-1.19 ms).
  - The 4x build's game frames match the earlier captures (0.00-0.02 mean abs).
  - The packaged `Meteor Blitz 8x.app` loads the 2048 terrain and renders World 3 at 4K.
  - `cargo test`: 26 passed.
  - The new PNGs were recompressed with oxipng, pixel-identical as RGBA.
- Mid-session the Mac briefly had no display, and 4K captures came out 3840x1018. Those runs were redone once the display was back.
- Not pushed.

## 8x background creases

- The user still saw tiling creases in the 8x build. Measuring the textures directly (the earlier screen-row scan cannot see seams on the curved mesh) showed the crossfade left the outer ~256 px of each 2048 texture at 56-70% of the centre's detail, a soft grid at every wrap.
- `tools/upscale8x.py` now upscales each original with 128 px of wrapped padding and crops the centre, so the edges continue into each other at full detail. `close_seam` spreads the original's own wrap step (fogs 1.5-1.8x a typical step) over a 128 px band. Alpha is upscaled as a separate grey image. The fogs use a padded Lanczos upscale, because Real-ESRGAN sharpened their brush smears into horizontal streaks.
- Worlds 4/5 still showed a thin cross. The original `craters.png` has alpha down to 216 on its 1-2 px border, and the backdrop multiplies by base alpha. The planet bases (`normalmaps.BASES`) are now written fully opaque.
- World 3's dark band is a dark lake in the original `green.png` that crosses its top/bottom wrap. It is content, continuous across the seam; the old 4x crossfade had blurred it away.
- Verified at 3840x2160 with `MB_HD=8`:
  - Worlds 1-6 recaptured with no straight seam lines.
  - Wrap step / typical step: 0.88-1.13 for the bases, 1.28-1.39 for the fogs (about one level).
  - World 4: 60 fps, draw 1.04 ms.
  - oxipng output is pixel-identical as RGBA.
  - `assets/8x` is 162 MB.
- Next: the 4x set has the same crossfade soft band and could get the same pipeline. Not pushed.
