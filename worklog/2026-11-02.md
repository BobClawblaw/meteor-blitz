## 8x background creases

- The user still saw tiling creases in the 8x build. Measuring the textures directly (the earlier screen-row scan cannot see seams on the curved mesh) showed the crossfade left the outer ~256 px of each 2048 texture at 56-70% of the centre's detail, a soft grid at every wrap.
- `tools/upscale8x.py` now upscales each original with 128 px of wrapped padding and crops the centre, so the edges continue into each other at full detail. `close_seam` spreads the original's own wrap step (fogs 1.5-1.8x a typical step) over a 128 px band. Alpha is upscaled as a separate grey image. The fogs use a padded Lanczos upscale, because Real-ESRGAN sharpened their brush smears into horizontal streaks.
- Worlds 4/5 still showed a thin cross. The original `craters.png` has alpha down to 216 on its 1-2 px border, and the backdrop multiplies by base alpha. The planet bases (`normalmaps.BASES`) are now written fully opaque.
- World 3's dark band is a dark lake in the original `green.png` that crosses its top/bottom wrap. It is content, continuous across the seam; the old 4x crossfade had blurred it away.
- Verified at 3840x2160 with `MB_HD=8`:
  - Worlds 1-6 recaptured with no straight seam lines.
  - Wrap step / typical step: 0.88-1.13 for the bases, 1.28-1.39 for the fogs (about one level).
  - World 4: 60 fps, draw 1.04 ms.
  - oxipng output is pixel-identical as RGBA.
  - `assets/8x` is 162 MB.
- Next: the 4x set has the same crossfade soft band and could get the same pipeline. Not pushed.

## Name entry fixes

- The user reported the name entry screen: black boxes behind the letters, and the caret floating above the name.
- Boxes: 20 of the 8x UI/sprite files had lost their transparency, including the font atlas, the HUD labels and the pause icon. oxipng stores the 4x sources as palette or grey+alpha PNGs, and `xbr_scale` only took the alpha path for RGBA. `xbr_scale` and `xbr4` now convert those modes to RGBA first; the 8x UI, sprites and font were rebuilt. Checked: every 4x file with transparency has it in its 8x copy.
- Caret: it was drawn at y 140-162, above the text, which hangs down from y 160. It is now drawn at the text's top, with the letters' ink height (13 rows of the 15-row glyphs; `Font::cap_height`), one glyph gap after the last letter. Checked in a 1080p capture with a test name.

## 8x default with 4096 terrain

- The user asked for the 8x art as the default, upscaled from the originals.
- Terrain is now 4096x4096 from the originals: Real-ESRGAN x4plus twice on the padded texture, brought down to 8x, then cropped. I compared it against Lanczos and the anime 2x model as the second step; double Real-ESRGAN drew the crispest edges. The originals are soft, so the gain over 2048 is modest.
- The seam pipeline now lives in `upscale4x.py` (`terrain`), and the 4x set's 2x terrain was rebuilt with it. That removes its crossfade soft band too. `close_seam` smoothing is now 1/512 of the size: wrap / typical step went from 1.38 to 1.03 on fogRed.
