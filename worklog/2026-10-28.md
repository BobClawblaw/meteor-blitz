## Menu fidelity measured against the emulator frame

- Extended `tools/compare_frame.py` with `--region NAME=x0,y0,x1,y1` so a run reports per-region diffs instead of only a whole-frame score.

Verified with real output:
- `tools/compare_frame.py original/screenshots/emu/main_menu.png --scan 0 480 40 --env MB_SHOT_FRAME=300 --region PLAY=... --region "HIGH SCORES=..." --region SETTINGS=... --region HELP=...`: best whole-frame diff 7.80 at scroll=120; per-region PLAY 0.06, HIGH SCORES 0.04, SETTINGS 0.07, HELP 0.10, i.e. the menu art and layout are pixel-exact against the original.

- Title template search: best match at x=4, y=15, confirming the port's title placement.
