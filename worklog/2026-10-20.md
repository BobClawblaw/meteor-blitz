## 06:40 — game.raw decoded properly: 346 named textures

## Rings and multiplier

- Changed: `src/bonus.rs` ports `Bonus`. A ring lives 5 s and fades over its last second. It spins at 1000°/s and wanders with drag 0.3. It homes in at 250 units/s once the ship is within 50 units, and is collected within 14.
  - Collecting a ring plays sound 9, starts point effect 15, adds one ring, and scores 1 times the multiplier.
  - Scored small rocks drop one ring, from Rock::setState(10007) through Enemy::activateBonuses with a spread of 5.

  - Pieces: radius 13, mass 10, health 20. They orbit 35 units out.
  - Pattern 0 chases and spins without ever throwing.
  - Pattern 1 spins up and releases pieces one at a time at the ship.
  - Pattern 2 throws them all toward the ship while they keep orbiting a moving centre.
  - Pattern 3 throws them all straight outward.
  - Pattern 4 spirals them out, then lets them go.
