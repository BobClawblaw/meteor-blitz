# 2026-11-01

## Resolution options up to 4K

- Added Options > Resolution: Window, 854x480, 1280x720, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 3840x2160 (4K). The choice persists as `settings.txt` `resolution` (`window` for the fitted size) and is applied at launch.
- A fixed preset resizes the window to that physical resolution and sets the offscreen canvas to the same size (`gfx::set_render_height`); Window keeps the old fitted-resolution behaviour. The target cap is 8 (4K fits a 6.75 scale).
- macOS window frames are in points and include the title bar. `gfx::set_window_resolution` divides by the backing scale and adds the measured title-bar height (via `NSApplication.windows` / `NSWindow.contentLayoutRect`); Windows and X11 take client pixels directly.
- Options layout: added the row after Fullscreen, reduced the main-row pitch from 23 to 21 so the 12 rows and the help line fit; the Options page was re-captured at 2560x1440, 3840x2160, 1280x720 and 854x480. All labels are legible and nothing overlaps.
- `MB_RESOLUTION=<WxH|window>` overrides the saved setting for a run (and resizes the window); `MB_WINDOW` still wins unless `MB_RESOLUTION` is set.

Verified with real output:
- `cargo test`: 26 passed (settings round-trip and resolution parsing/reset; resolution cycle, labels, and row-layout fit).
- `MB_RESOLUTION=<size> MB_MENU=options MB_SHOT=...` captures are exactly 854x480, 1280x720, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 3840x2160 (PIL image sizes), and the 4K Options capture shows `RESOLUTION 3840X2160` with clean spacing.
- 4K gameplay (`MB_RESOLUTION=3840x2160 MB_START=1.1 MB_SHOT=...`) renders at 3840x2160; the HUD and ship are visible.
- 4K performance (`MB_PROF=1 MB_PROF_FRAMES=240 MB_FPS=60`): draw 0.77 ms average / 1.10 ms max after the first frame, frame 16.67 ms (60.0 fps).
- `MB_DUMP_SETTINGS=1` prints `resolution=3840x2160` with `MB_RESOLUTION`, and `resolution=window` without it.

The running harness reloaded the config, and the screenshots above were inspected visually.
