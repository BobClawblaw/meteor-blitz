## Physics particles keep the original effects

- The user: the Physics style was meant to improve the explosion graphics, not remove the original ones. It replaced them: `Play::fx` returned no effect, and the shot hits, the ship's death, bombs and a broken shield ran either the physics particles or the original sprite systems. Now the original effects always play and Physics adds its particles on top. `fx` and the 1.1.2 `Effects::start_hidden` workaround for the boss's death timer are gone, since the charge-up now simply plays.
- Verified: `cargo test` 38 passed; with Physics on, stage 1.1's boss dies and the run advances (`advance: ok true`, score 15400); frames 330-480 of stage 1.1 show the original shards and sparks over the physics fire and smoke; 4K stage 4.1 at zoom 1.5 draws in 0.90 ms on average with Physics (both layers) against 0.79 ms Classic, both at 60 fps. Test runs used a dummy `MB_ROOT` so the repo's save files were not written.
