# Meteor Blitz 1.0.0

A love letter to Alley Labs' *Meteor Blitz* (iOS, 2009), given away freely and remade in Rust through AI-assisted reverse engineering. The original's logic, tuning, waves and bosses, with a modern presentation.

## Downloads

- **macOS** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.0-macos.dmg`): universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. Open the DMG and drag Meteor Blitz to Applications. The app is not notarised, so on first launch macOS asks for permission: right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings > Privacy & Security.
- **Windows** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.0-windows.zip`): unzip anywhere and run `MeteorBlitz.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app; choose More info > Run anyway.

Everything the game needs ships with it. Settings, bindings and scores are saved to `~/Library/Application Support/Meteor Blitz` (macOS) or `%APPDATA%\Meteor Blitz` (Windows).

## What's in it

- All 30 arcade stages, survival mode, every enemy and the boss, ported from the original binary.
- Widescreen and ultrawide, resizable window, fullscreen (F11 or Alt+Enter).
- 60, 120 or 144 Hz with interpolated frames.
- Dynamic lighting with normal-mapped terrain, emitter bloom, shockwaves, per-world colour grading, scorch marks, soft particles and ship effects.
- HD art, an Options screen, rebindable keyboard/mouse and controller bindings, and the original tutorial (optional).
- Xbox controllers on macOS and Windows.
