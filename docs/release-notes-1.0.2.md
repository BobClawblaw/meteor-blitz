# Meteor Blitz 1.0.2

**Survival mode is the real thing.** Survival used to play a fixed set of world 6 waves and then end like a cleared stage. It is now ported from the original game's wave manager:

- It never ends on its own: survive until your lives run out, then enter your name for the survival table.
- Waves come from the original's 114-wave survival pool, picked at random against a difficulty budget that grows as you go. Harder waves only appear as the budget rises.
- The game keeps the pressure on: it adds waves until the field is near the budget, so clearing the screen fast doesn't buy a breather.
- A supply depot drops in on wave 8 and every 15 waves after it, followed by a short pause.
- Dying, using a bomb or losing your shield eases the difficulty back off.
- Each wave speeds the game up slightly.

The web version (https://bobclawblaw.github.io/meteor-blitz/) already has this.

## Downloads

- **macOS** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.2-macos.dmg`): universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. Open the DMG and drag Meteor Blitz to Applications. The app is not notarised, so on first launch macOS asks for permission: right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings > Privacy & Security.
- **Windows** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.2-windows.zip`): unzip anywhere and run `MeteorBlitz.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app; choose More info > Run anyway.

Settings, bindings and scores carry over from earlier versions.
