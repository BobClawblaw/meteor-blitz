# Meteor Blitz 1.0.9

- **Off-screen indicators.** Enemies beyond the edge of the screen get the original's small red arrow, 100 units from the ship and pointing at them, as in the iPhone game: rocks, mines, towers, lobbers, seekers, arrows, snakes, shy guys and the boss (whose arrow is twice the size). They show while 12 or fewer enemies are on the field; rings, supply depots and snake bodies never get one. On a widescreen window they appear only past its wider edge.
- **Version on the main menu.** The lower right corner shows the version and the date it was built, e.g. `v1.0.9 2026-11-02`.
- **Controls page at 3:2.** In the original's aspect the controller column no longer runs off the right edge.

The web version (https://bobclawblaw.github.io/meteor-blitz/) has all three.

## Downloads

- **macOS** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.9-macos.dmg`): universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. Open the DMG and drag Meteor Blitz to Applications. The app is not notarised, so on first launch macOS asks for permission: right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings > Privacy & Security.
- **Windows** (`MeteorBlitz-1.0.9-windows.zip`): unzip anywhere and run `MeteorBlitz.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app; choose More info > Run anyway.

Settings, bindings and scores carry over from earlier versions.
